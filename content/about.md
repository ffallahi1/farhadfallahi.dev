I’m an undergraduate Cybersecurity student at York University with a strong interest in network security, penetration testing, and security-focused programming. My goal is to build practical skills that allow me to understand, identify, and address real-world security challenges.

Throughout my studies and independent learning, I have completed coursework and training covering CCNA, Network+, Security+, and CEH concepts. Alongside my cybersecurity knowledge, I am proficient in Python and enjoy using programming to automate tasks, analyze systems, and explore security-related problems.

A major part of my learning comes from hands-on experience. I have built and worked on various home labs focused on networking, penetration testing, and applying Python to cybersecurity scenarios. These projects have allowed me to move beyond theoretical concepts and develop practical troubleshooting, problem-solving, and technical skills in a controlled environment.

I’m continuously learning and expanding my knowledge of cybersecurity, with a particular interest in gaining deeper experience through projects, labs, and real-world challenges. I’m excited to connect with others in the field and continue developing as a cybersecurity professional.
